What we process, and why

Account data — first name, last name, e-mail address, role
To create and run your account and to show trainers who is in their group. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Stored until you delete your account.
Groups, memberships and invitations
So trainers can release quizzes to a defined circle of people. An invitation stores the e-mail address it was sent to. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Deleted when the group or the membership is removed.
Quizzes and completion counts
Quiz content belongs to the trainer who created it. How often you completed a quiz is stored on your own device in the mobile app and is not transmitted to us.
Test results — pass/fail, score in percent, attempts, time
When a trainer releases a test to a group you are in and you take it, the result of each attempt is stored: whether you passed, your score in percent, the attempt number and when you took it. The trainer who owns the test can see these results for the trainees in their groups, so they can tell who has passed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (carrying out the training relationship). The score is a plain comparison against the pass mark set by the trainer, not an automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). Results are kept until the test is deleted, until the trainer resets your attempts, or until you delete your account, whichever comes first.
Invitation e-mails
When a trainer invites someone to a group, that e-mail address receives one notice telling them who invited them and to which group. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in letting an invited person know). The mail is sent from our own mail server — no external mail provider is involved, so no data is passed to a third party. If the invitation is declined or removed, the address is deleted with it.

Cookies

One cookie, qm_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the login to work, so no consent is required (§ 165(3) TKG 2021). It is signed, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and expires after 14 days at the latest.

There are no analytics, tracking, advertising or profiling cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or comparable tools.

No third-party requests

All fonts, styles and scripts are served from this server. Nothing is loaded from external sources such as Google Fonts or a CDN, so your IP address is not disclosed to any third party when you use this site. A Content Security Policy enforces this technically (Art. 25 GDPR, data protection by design).
